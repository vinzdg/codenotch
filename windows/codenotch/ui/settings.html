Open Codenotch at login
Codenotch opens by itself every time you sign in to this computer, and waits quietly in the background until a coding session starts. Turn it off and you have to open Codenotch yourself.

Updates
Codenotch looks for a new version shortly after it starts, and can install it for you. The download comes from GitHub, and its signature is checked before anything is run.
Data folder
Everything Codenotch remembers lives in one folder: your settings, its log file, and the "glyphs" folder where you can drop your own icons. Opens in File Explorer.
