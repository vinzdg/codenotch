Codedock: a pill beside the Dock to talk to your Claude Code sessions without leaving what you are doing.

  • Codedock: turn it on in Settings → Appearance → Codedock. It shows the session's usage ring and what Claude is doing right now, and sends what you type straight into its terminal: VS Code, Cursor, Terminal, iTerm2 and more.
  • Every session, one click away: step through them by repository, or open the list grouped by repository and branch.
  • Slash commands, questions and the conversation: type / for Claude Code's commands, /model and others with their options; answer Claude's questions; open the latest exchange; stop a turn with Esc.
  • Images inline: paste or drop an image and it sits in the sentence as a small frame.
  • Your look: Dark, Match the Dock, Flat Black or Flat White, left or right of the Dock.
  • A Claude session with a shell left running in the background now reads as ready, not working.
